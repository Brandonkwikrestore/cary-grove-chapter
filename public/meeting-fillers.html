⏱ Meeting Fillers
Use these when a spotlight speaker cancels, the agenda runs short, or you need to fill 3–5 minutes with something genuinely valuable.
1
Referral Hot Seat
Pick one member. The room has 60 seconds to brainstorm everyone they know who could use that member's service right now. Go around fast.
2
Trigger Phrase Drill
Read a trigger phrase out loud. Members shout whose name comes to mind. Repeat for 5 phrases. Fast, energizing, reinforces referral awareness.
3
Best Referral Story
Ask: "Who has a referral story — given or received — that made a real difference?" One or two members share in 90 seconds each.
4
Power Partner Mapping
Ask two members to stand. The room identifies every situation where their clients overlap. Usually generates 2–3 warm referrals on the spot.
5
60-Second Commercial Workshop
Volunteer rewrites their commercial live using the Problem / Guide / Outcome structure. The room gives feedback. Always valuable.
6
Guest Introduction Round
If guests are present, give each 60 seconds to introduce themselves and say what kind of connections they are looking for. Warms the room immediately.
7
Referral Etiquette Check-In
Ask: "Who received a referral this month? Did you follow up within 24 hours? Did you close the loop with the member who sent it?" Quick accountability round.
8
One-on-One Challenge
Ask members to pair up with someone they have not met with recently. Schedule a one-on-one before next Tuesday. Takes 2 minutes, generates real activity all week.
9
Chapter Goal Review
Pull up the referral report. Share where the chapter stands vs. the monthly goal. Celebrate wins. Name the gap. Two minutes of real accountability.
10
Ideal Client Clarification
Ask one member: "Who is your single best client type this month — the one referral that would change your quarter?" The room listens and takes notes.
11
Thank You Round
Ask: "Who wants to publicly thank a member for a referral, connection, or support this month?" Builds culture and recognition in under 3 minutes.
12
P.O.W.E.R. Recitation
Read through the P.O.W.E.R. mantra together. Ask: "Which letter did you execute well this week? Which one needs work?" Quick and grounding.
