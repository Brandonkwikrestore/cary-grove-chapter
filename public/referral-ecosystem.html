💡 The P.O.W.E.R. Referral Mantra
P
Personal

Use first-person language: "I personally recommend…" The more personal the introduction, the more powerful the referral.

O
Opportunity

Frame the referral as a gift: "This is someone who can genuinely help you." Raise the value of the connection for both people.

W
Win

A great referral produces a win for all three people: you as the connector, the chapter member gaining a prospect, and the person being referred.

E
Effective

Make the connection happen. Send an email, text an introduction, or personally introduce them. Follow up within 48 hours.

R
Respect

Every referral involves three reputations. Update the referring member. Close the loop. Say thank you — every time, without exception.

📖 What Is a Qualified Referral?

A qualified referral is a warm introduction to a specific person who has an expressed need for a chapter member's service — and who has been told to expect a call. It is not a lead, a name drop, or a business card hand-off.

"I personally want to introduce you to someone I trust completely. She specializes in exactly what you're dealing with, and I've already told her to expect your call."
